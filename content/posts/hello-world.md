Every programmer's first program is "Hello, World." So it feels right to start here.

I've been meaning to start a blog for a while. I have a lot of half-formed thoughts scattered across notes apps, scraps of paper, and the back of my mind. This is the place where they graduate into full sentences.

## What this blog is about

Mostly software — things I'm building, things I'm learning, tools I find useful or frustrating. But I'll also write about ideas that don't fit neatly into a category. That's the point of having your own corner of the internet.

## Why Hugo and GitHub Pages

I wanted something I control. No algorithmic feed, no paywall, no ads. Just markdown files and a static site generator. Hugo is fast, PaperMod is clean, and GitHub Pages is free. Done.

If you've stumbled here somehow — welcome. More soon.
